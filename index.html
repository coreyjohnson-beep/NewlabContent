The Founders — 1-Min Format Playbook
Eight Prompts.
One Builder
Ethos.
Easily replicable one-minute founder videos built on prompt-based, not personality-based formats. Each follows a 3-part rhythm — setup, moment, reflection — making editing standardized and scalable across all five locations. No deep domain expertise required from the interviewer. Universal structure, local flavor.

Format Prompts (8)

What's Your Jig?
Format 01 · Visual Rich
Every engineer has had the moment when ingenuity meets improvisation — when something had to be rigged to make an idea work. Founders tell the story of a time they hacked together a prototype or improvised a solution to get their technology off the ground.
Props + DemoUniversally RelatableHumor Potential

Three Things
Format 02 · Object-Based
Founders share three objects in their workspace that symbolize their journey — a burnt-out circuit board, a first prototype, a childhood artifact. Each object anchors a story and gives natural cutaway footage. Portable — can be shot anywhere.
Natural B-RollSelf-GuidedEmotionally Resonant

Inception
Format 03 · Origin Story
Founders reflect on their earliest memory related to the technology or field they now work in — the moment the spark was lit. A space launch on TV, a broken radio they tried to fix, a science fair gone wrong. Low technical barrier. Works across all disciplines.

Breakthrough Moment
Format 04 · Dramatic Arc
The defining moment — the instant when a problem, experiment, or idea suddenly clicked. The time the founder first knew their technology could actually work. Centers the story around a clear turning point. Blends personal emotion with a moment of engineering insight.

In the Wild
Format 05 · Field Notes
Innovation doesn't live only in the lab. Founders show or describe how their technology interacts with the real world — a surprising user, a field test gone wrong, a deployment that revealed something new. Grounds technology in context. Authentic field footage.

Show not just tell — the universal thread across all eight formats
The most consistent theme in high-performing reference content is "show not just tell" quality — artists presenting their work, scientists describing in-depth processes with visual aids. Every prompt in this playbook is designed to generate that quality naturally, without requiring an interviewer with deep domain expertise. All eight formats: prompt-based not personality-based, visually modular with natural b-roll opportunities, follow the universal 3-part setup/moment/reflection structure, and maintain cultural cohesion across all five locations while allowing each space's local flavor to emerge organically.

Two extremes are performing — raw iPhone and cinematic T1 — with little in between
Jan–Feb 2026 performance and the broader 2025 content review both confirm: what's doing well is either raw, unedited personality-driven video (T3) or highly cinematic production-heavy content (T1). The common thread is serialization — people want to follow along and come back for the next episode. T2 is the hardest tier to execute well because it has to be polished but feel genuine. The Founders 1-min format playbook is specifically designed to operate in T2 / T3 territory — artful but replicable, structured but personal.
